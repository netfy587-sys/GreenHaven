04

Make Every Click Count

At GreenHaven we believe the greenest product is often the one you already own. Before you buy or follow any green tip online, run these four checks.

1

Do I need it?

Use what you have first. Buying less is the simplest saving.

2

Is there proof?

Look for clear numbers, ratings or certifications, not vague claims like "eco".

3

Will it last?

Durable, repairable items usually beat cheap ones replaced often.

4

Can I keep doing it?

A small habit you stick with beats a big change you drop.
